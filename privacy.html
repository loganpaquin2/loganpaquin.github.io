Privacy Policy

OpenBot Staff Connector. Effective November 9, 2026.

What this app is

OpenBot Staff Connector is a private tool used only by Logan Paquin and his own staff accounts. It helps manage Logan's own email and calendars. It is not offered to the public.

What it accesses

With the account owner's permission, the app can read email, send email, read calendars, and create or change calendar events, only for the Google accounts that Logan connects himself.

How data is used and stored

  • Data is used only to read, organize, and respond to Logan's own email and schedule.
  • Access tokens are stored encrypted on a computer Logan controls. Nothing is stored on a public server.
  • Data is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.
  • Emails are sent only after Logan approves each message.

Google user data

The app's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time from Google Account, Third-party apps and services. Removing it stops all access immediately.
